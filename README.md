# ArtViSiON — Ranjan (Rj) Sarkar · Portfolio

Creative Lead portfolio site for **ArtViSiON** (Indore, since 2024).
*Your ViSiON, Our Creation.*

Static site: plain HTML + CSS + JavaScript. No build step, no dependencies.

## Files
- `index.html` — page structure
- `css/style.css` — styles
- `js/app.js` — interactions (work grid, case views, A to Z full-screen viewer, scroll storyboard)
- `404.html`, `robots.txt` — GitHub Pages not-found page and crawler rules
- `assets/` — logo, fonts, project covers and A–Z / storyboard SVG artwork

## Artwork (all original vector SVG, flat style)
- `assets/covers/` — feature image (ACP-clad brand store) + 8 project covers
- `assets/dive/` — 9 scenes for the scroll-dive (ACP facade, coffee, poster wall, botanical, book, social grid, mark construction, brand store interior, POSM)
- `assets/storyboard/` — 5 pillar scenes, golden-ratio key visual, A to Z letters (A–Z)
Every image is used once; the only intentional repeat is the work-grid cover / list hover preview.

## Artwork slideshow
The full-width slideshow under the hero shows artwork that appears nowhere else on the page with left/right arrows, thumbnails, keyboard arrows (focus the slideshow), swipe and gentle autoplay (pauses on hover). Slide 1 is the ACP cladding signage & brand store branding artwork, slide 2 the 360° mall branding presentation, then the 10 mobile-brand retail scenes (`assets/retail/`) and the 360° expo pavilion. Project covers, scroll-dive scenes, pillar scenes and A to Z are not repeated here. To add a slide, add one entry to `slides` in `js/app.js`.

## Device optimisation
- Tested at 320, 360, 390, 430, 600, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920 and 2560 px wide, plus a phone in landscape: no horizontal scroll, no console errors, no 404s.
- Touch screens: all buttons, chips and links are at least 44 x 44 px; hover-only effects do not stick after a tap; slideshow swipes horizontally while the page still scrolls vertically.
- Notched phones: `viewport-fit=cover` with safe-area padding. Home-screen icon and web-app meta tags are included.
- Short landscape screens: two-column hero and a height-capped slideshow.
- Large monitors (1800 px and up): the layout widens in steps instead of floating in the middle.
- Speed: images below the fold are lazy-loaded, the first slide has `fetchpriority=high`, fonts are `woff2` with `font-display:swap`, and slideshow autoplay pauses off-screen, in background tabs, with reduced motion and with Data Saver on.
- Pillars section: the accordion works on every device (hover on desktop, tap on touch); on phones and tablets it switches instantly so the page does not jump.

## Artwork animation
Every artwork SVG (75 files in `assets/`) loops a small animation, so the images play one after another:
- the shapes pop in a wave from left to right (they grow slightly from their base, so objects stay on the floor),
- the ArtViSiON logos pulse,
- the people sway gently.

It is pure CSS inside each SVG (works inside `<img>`) and stops automatically when the visitor has "reduce motion" switched on. The un-animated originals are in `work/backup_anim/` (not in the zip); `work/animate_svgs.py` rebuilds the animation from them.

## Hover artworks
On desktop, hovering any artwork (Work cards, A to Z) reveals a different minimal vector scene with a circular wipe. 14 minimal flat vector scenes are in `assets/hover/` (cycled over the images, never two alike side by side). Generated by `work/hover_art2.py` (map in `work/hover_map.json`). On touch screens (no hover) the artworks peek by themselves, one at a time, while they are on screen; it is off when "reduce motion" is on.

## Logo on red
Where the logo sits on a red background (9 artworks), its beak is white, so it stays visible. Done with a CSS variable in each SVG (`--bk`); `work/beak_white.py` finds the red backgrounds and then `work/animate_svgs.py` rebuilds the files.
