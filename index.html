    05 — Method

    The rules behind the work.

    Nothing on this page is arbitrary. It is built on a 12-column grid, an 8-point spacing scale and a type scale that grows by the golden ratio. Press G to see the grid, B for the baseline.

    01 / Hierarchy× 1.618
    AaAaAaAaAa

    Scale & hierarchy

    Every size is the previous one × φ. Big contrast between steps makes the reading order obvious before a single word is read. A serif display is paired with a geometric sans for text.

    On this page: 16 → 26 → 42 → 68 → 110 px headings.
    02 / Structure12 · 24 · 1320

    Grid & alignment

    Twelve columns, a 24 px gutter. Content spans 7+5 and 5+7 — asymmetric, yet every edge sits on a column line. That is how variety stays orderly.

    Try it: press G and look at the Work grid.
    03 / Colour60 · 30 · 10
    60 neutral30 ink10 accent

    Colour & contrast

    Cream carries the page, ink gives weight, and the accent — taken from the logo — is used sparingly, so it always means “look here”. Ratios are measured live.

    How we see

    The eye groups what is close, alike, almost complete or flowing. The ArtViSiON mark is pure closure — two open eyes and a beak are enough for a face. Hover to complete the loop.

    On this page: the logo’s eyes in the hero.
    05 / EmphasisIsolation

    One focal point

    When one element differs from many, it wins attention instantly (the Von Restorff effect). So there is one red triangle per scene — never two competing accents.

    On this page: the red triangle in every section label.

    Space & rhythm

    Every margin and gap is a multiple of 8. White space is not empty — it groups, separates and gives the work room to feel expensive. Body text runs at 16/26 and ~56 characters per line.

    Try it: press B to reveal the 8-pt baseline.

    07 / Composition

    Thirds & the golden section

    Every hero image is composed so the focal point lands on a power point or inside the spiral, with leading lines pulling the eye toward it and calm negative space around. Switch the overlay to check.
