    01 — Art theory

    What actually sits inside a rectangle, and why the eye believes it.

    6 rules

    Art 011 / 6

    The seven elements

    Line, shape, form, space, tone, colour, texture.

    Every image ever made is only these, arranged.

    On the job room posters · POSM · murals

    Art 022 / 6

    Line, shape, form

    Line divides, shape fills, tone turns a shape into form.

    A 2D drawing becomes a sign the moment it shows a side.

    On the job signage design · dielines · mockups

    Art 033 / 6

    Abstraction

    Decide early how far the artwork sits from reality.

    Far away reads fast from a road; close up reads in a hand.

    On the job hoarding art · packaging · icons

    Art 044 / 6

    Negative space

    The gap is an element too. Design the hole.

    Crowd a mark and the mark disappears.

    On the job logo lockups · fascia layout · vinyl

    Art 055 / 6

    Movement & eye path

    Block the attention, hold a silence, then one call to act.

    The eye follows the Z: top-left, across, down, back.

    On the job poster walls · standees · window display

    Art 066 / 6

    Unity & variety

    Unity holds a set together; variety tells the eye where to look.

    One thing must break the rule, and only one.

    On the job campaign kits · menu boards · zonal signage

    02 — Advertising theory

    A board is not a picture. It is a three-second argument.

    6 rules

    Advertising 011 / 6

    AIDA

    Attention, interest, desire, action. In that order.

    Skip a gate and the money is spent twice.

    On the job hoardings · mall media · feed ads

    Advertising 022 / 6

    The 3-second test

    Who is it for, what is it, where do I act.

    If a stranger cannot answer, it is decoration.

    On the job roadside · transit · shop window

    Advertising 033 / 6

    40 / 40 / 20

    Forty per cent hook, forty per cent proof, twenty per cent action.

    Most Indian boards lose the action band to a bigger logo.

    On the job hoarding art · standee · banner

    Advertising 044 / 6

    Reach vs frequency

    Widen or repeat. Pick one per budget.

    Reach introduces; frequency makes people believe.

    On the job radio-route boards · auto branding · mall

    Advertising 055 / 6

    One promise

    One benefit, one audience, one action.

    A line a stranger can repeat after seeing it once.

    On the job fascia headline · POSM · campaign line

    Advertising 066 / 6

    Recall device

    One repeating shape, colour or word, in every placement.

    Repetition is how a small brand buys memory.

    On the job retail rollouts · events · social

    03 — Branding theory

    A brand is a system that still works when you are not in the room.

    6 rules

    Branding 011 / 6

    The identity system

    Mark, type, colour, pattern, rules. Five parts, one idea.

    A logo alone is not an identity; it is a signature.

    On the job brand book · shop kit · packaging

    Branding 022 / 6

    Positioning: one word

    Own one word in the customer's head.

    Own five and you own none.

    On the job market board · fascia · campaign

    Branding 033 / 6

    Brand architecture

    Decide which name carries the trust and which ones ride on it.

    Every sub-brand costs money to teach.

    On the job multi-brand groups · store fascias

    Branding 044 / 6

    Consistency

    Same cue at every size and on every surface.

    A mark is a promise kept in public.

    On the job rollout · packaging · vehicles

    Branding 055 / 6

    Voice & tone

    Voice is the words; tone is the mood they arrive in.

    One voice across the counter, the board and the app.

    On the job copy on signage · POSM · WhatsApp

    Branding 066 / 6

    Touchpoints

    Map every surface the brand will touch, then kit them together.

    Where the brand is seen decides what must be designed.

    On the job storefront to stationery, one kit

    04 — Colour theory

    Colour is a system for reading a scene, not a mood board.

    8 rules

    Colour 011 / 8

    The wheel & 60 · 30 · 10

    One dominant, one support, one accent.

    The accent is a pointer, not a decoration.

    On the job fascia · packaging · print collateral

    Colour 022 / 8

    Harmony recipes

    Mono, analogous, complementary, split, triad.

    Five safe recipes; pick one and stay inside it.

    On the job brand palette · seasonal campaigns

    Colour 033 / 8

    Value before hue

    Squint and the layout must still work in black and white.

    Value does the reading; hue does the feeling.

    On the job signage contrast · vinyl · POSM

    Colour 044 / 8

    Saturation ladder

    Neutral sells the product; vivid sells the offer.

    Saturation is the volume knob, not the quality.

    On the job price flashes · offer panels

    Colour 055 / 8

    Warm & cool

    Warm advances, cool recedes.

    Two warms arguing in one panel read as noise.

    On the job store lighting · backdrop · packaging

    Colour 066 / 8

    Colour meaning

    Every colour already has a job in the customer's head.

    Use the meaning; do not fight it.

    On the job offer colours · safety · brand zones

    Colour 077 / 8

    Contrast ratio

    4.5 : 1 is the floor for text, 7 : 1 for a board in sun.

    Contrast is a number, not an opinion.

    On the job small print · terms · wayfinding

    Colour 088 / 8

    Screen, print, spot

    Screen, CMYK and spot colour are three different colours.

    Pick the print colour before the client approves the mockup.

    On the job colour matching · material samples

    05 — Texture theory

    Finish is a message the customer feels before reading.

    6 rules

    Texture 011 / 6

    Four finishes

    Gloss, matte, embossed, brushed.

    One colour, four prices and four readings.

    On the job lamination · letters · sheet metal

    Texture 022 / 6

    Highlight & light

    Products read satin, wayfinding reads matte, shop fronts read gloss.

    The highlight is the whole difference.

    On the job material selection · night look

    Texture 033 / 6

    Texture at true scale

    The same file is cloth at 1 m and noise at 10 cm.

    Design texture at final size; print a 1:1 strip.

    On the job wall graphics · packaging · backdrops

    Texture 044 / 6

    Texture vs text

    Put texture where the message is not.

    Texture and text fight for the same pixels.

    On the job posters · menu boards · window vinyl

    Texture 055 / 6

    Finish as signal

    Matte signals premium; gloss signals value.

    One finish per job; mix only to separate zones.

    On the job category zones · store-in-store

    Texture 066 / 6

    Printed vs real

    Print fakes texture in ink; fabrication owns it in the material.

    Mock the real finish with a sample, not a filter.

    On the job client approvals · samples board

    06 — Typography theory

    Type is a measuring instrument as well as a voice.

    10 rules

    Typography 011 / 10

    Anatomy

    Cap height, x-height, counter, terminal, baseline.

    Name the parts and the feedback gets shorter.

    On the job logo drawing · type criticism

    Typography 022 / 10

    Four families

    Serif, sans, slab, mono. Each one carries a tone.

    Two families is a system; four is a folder of accidents.

    On the job brand book · signage · packaging

    Typography 033 / 10

    Hierarchy in four levels

    Eyebrow, heading, body, caption.

    A stranger should be able to rank them instantly.

    On the job boards · brochures · POSM

    Typography 044 / 10

    Line length

    Sixty to seventy characters per line.

    Line length is comfort, not style.

    On the job letters · brochures · terms

    Typography 055 / 10

    Leading & line gap

    Body copy sits at 1.5 to 1.7 of the type size.

    Headlines pull tighter as they grow.

    On the job documents · standees · panels

    Typography 066 / 10

    Tracking

    Small text needs air; large text needs tightening.

    Caps on signs need +10 per cent, not zero.

    On the job fascia · wayfinding · badges

    Typography 077 / 10

    Pairing

    Pair by contrast: a serif head with a sans body.

    Same size and weight, different skeleton, is a safe pair.

    On the job brand book · boards · menus

    Typography 088 / 10

    Latin + Devanagari

    Match the weight, then the optics, not the point size.

    Hindi usually needs about 15 per cent more apparent size.

    On the job all Indian signage · packaging

    Typography 099 / 10

    Letter height by distance

    25 mm of capital height per metre of viewing distance.

    Halve the distance and halve the letters.

    On the job fascia · hoarding · wayfinding

    Typography 1010 / 10

    Numbers & phone numbers

    The number is the only line that must be read correctly.

    Bold, dark, spaced in pairs. Never over texture.

    On the job boards · POSM · vehicles

    07 — Pattern theory

    A pattern is one shape plus one rule, repeated until it becomes a surface.

    6 rules

    Pattern 011 / 6

    One shape, one rule

    Repeat, mirror or offset. Nothing else.

    Change the rule and the whole wall changes.

    On the job wall graphics · packaging · fabric

    Pattern 022 / 6

    Six pattern families

    Dots, stripes, chevron, diamond, hex, scales.

    The family sets the temperature of the surface.

    On the job store walls · kiosk cladding · cartons

    Pattern 033 / 6

    Rhythm & break

    A repeat with no break reads as a floor tile.

    Every few beats, make one decision.

    On the job feature walls · road graphics

    Pattern 044 / 6

    Pattern scale

    Scale decides whether it reads as texture or as pattern.

    Test it at the size it will be built.

    On the job ACP cladding · vinyl · interior panels

    Pattern 055 / 6

    Set-out on the wall

    Set out from a datum, not from the corner.

    Full tiles at the visible corner, slivers folded into waste.

    On the job shop interiors · exhibition booths

    Pattern 066 / 6

    Seamless repeat

    A pattern must survive being cut and joined.

    Mirror the edge or clip the motif, then check the seam.

    On the job backdrops · vinyl wraps · print runs

    08 — Vector theory

    A vector is a promise: the drawing stays true at any size the job needs.

    7 rules

    Vector 011 / 7

    Nodes & handles

    Two anchors, two handles, one clean path.

    Every extra node is a place the cut file can wobble.

    On the job logo drawing · cut art

    Vector 022 / 7

    Lines, arcs, curves

    Prefer the fewest segments that hold the shape.

    Fewer nodes mean a faster CNC and a cleaner cut.

    On the job CNC · laser · stitching

    Vector 033 / 7

    Corners & radii

    Pick one radius and keep it across the kit.

    Mixed radii read as an unfinished drawing.

    On the job panels · die lines · UI frames

    Vector 044 / 7

    Offset & stroke

    A CNC cuts the centre of the tool path, so expand every stroke.

    Offset by at least half the tool diameter.

    On the job acrylic letters · metal letters

    Vector 055 / 7

    Print art vs cut path

    One drawing, three files: what prints, what cuts, what falls away.

    Colour layers are not cut layers.

    On the job profiles · keylines · stickers

    Vector 066 / 7

    Clean-file checks

    Double nodes, open paths and stray slivers must go.

    These faults only show up on the machine.

    On the job every file that leaves the laptop

    Vector 077 / 7

    Export rules

    Print, cut, web and scale each get their own file.

    The wrong export is the most expensive mistake.

    On the job handover · vendor drop · archive

    09 — Measurement

    On site, a millimetre is money. Measure twice, cut once.

    8 rules

    Measure 011 / 8

    The recce sheet

    Width, height, level, supply, access, hours.

    Photograph every dimension with a tape in frame.

    On the job every job, day one

    Measure 022 / 8

    Fascia sizing

    Height follows length and letter count, not taste.

    Leave 120 mm of margin at both ends.

    On the job shop fascia · brand stores

    Measure 033 / 8

    Reading distance

    Pick letter height from the worst-case distance.

    Traffic speed, clutter and angle all cost size.

    On the job hoardings · mall fascia

    Measure 044 / 8

    The 25 mm rule

    25 mm of cap height per metre of viewing distance.

    Mall 20, road 25-30, highway 30-40 mm per metre.

    On the job all signage

    Measure 055 / 8

    Clearances

    2.4 m to walk under, 600 mm clear on top.

    Design the empty space as carefully as the sign.

    On the job fascia boxes · totems · banners

    Measure 066 / 8

    Glazing & vinyl

    Keep about 20 per cent of the glass for graphics.

    Door glass stays clear; safety stripe always.

    On the job shop front · mall stores

    Measure 077 / 8

    Totem & pylon

    Two audiences at two distances: pedestrian and vehicle.

    Divide the height; never stack two logos.

    On the job mall entry · petrol forecourt

    Measure 088 / 8

    The shop drawing

    One datum, dimensions off the face, one tolerance.

    The fabricator should never have to guess.

    On the job fabrication handover

    10 — Material

    The material is part of the design. Choose it before the artwork is signed.

    5 rules

    Material 011 / 5

    ACP sheet

    Two aluminium skins on a core, riveted to a sub-frame.

    Skins 0.25 mm, total 4 mm, FR grade near escapes.

    On the job cladding · fascia boxes · pillar covers

    Material 022 / 5

    Vinyl & film

    Cast film for curves and heat; calendared for flat and cheap.

    Ask for the brand and the warranty in writing.

    On the job glazing · walls · vehicles

    Material 033 / 5

    LED & power

    Load the supply to 80 per cent and ventilate the box.

    Backlit boards fail at the driver, not the letters.

    On the job lit fascia · letters · totems

    Material 044 / 5

    Fixing methods

    Rivets where you can reach the back, tape and silicone on glass.

    No fixing ever goes through the printed face.

    On the job cladding · wall signs · letters

    Material 055 / 5

    Boards & substrates

    MDF, WPC, foam PVC, ply. Each one has one job.

    Board is the cheapest part and the most expensive mistake.

    On the job indoor signs · panels · mockups

    11 — Production

    Production is where the drawing meets the machine. Gate every stage.

    6 rules

    Production 011 / 6

    The pipeline

    Recce, design, artwork, file check, cut, finish, install.

    No stage starts while the previous gate is open.

    On the job every job, every time

    Production 022 / 6

    Pre-press checks

    Bleed, outlined fonts, CMYK, DXF 1:1, plotted proof.

    Most site disasters are file disasters.

    On the job before any file leaves the laptop

    Production 033 / 6

    Cutting methods

    CNC 3 mm, laser 0.2, waterjet 0.9, hand 1.5 of kerf.

    No tool cuts a sharp inside corner.

    On the job ACP · acrylic · metal letters

    Production 044 / 6

    Printing methods

    UV flatbed, sublimation, screen, eco-solvent.

    Pick from the material and the run length, not the price.

    On the job boards · fabric · vinyl · runs

    Production 055 / 6

    QC sample

    Nothing is signed off on a screen.

    A sample settles arguments faster than a meeting.

    On the job colour · logo · alignment

    Production 066 / 6

    Packing & dispatch

    One box per wall, labelled by drawing number.

    Boards travel better than they look if packed for it.

    On the job multi-site rollouts

    12 — Machine learning

    AI is a fast junior with no site knowledge. Use it where it is honest.

    6 rules

    Machine learning 011 / 6

    What it can and cannot do

    It can trace, clean, resize, vary and draft.

    It cannot take site risk, price a job or own a copyright.

    On the job daily studio work

    Machine learning 022 / 6

    The six-line brief

    Who, what, where, how, don'ts, output format.

    Six lines beat a paragraph. Set the don'ts first.

    On the job any AI draft for a client

    Machine learning 033 / 6

    Photo to cut file

    Auto-trace gives a shape; a human gives a cut file.

    Budget the node clean-up, not the trace.

    On the job logos · profiles · graphics

    Machine learning 044 / 6

    Mockups on the real site

    Mock the artwork onto the actual site photo.

    Never ask a client to imagine the result.

    On the job approvals · pitches

    Machine learning 055 / 6

    Variants & testing

    Generate for volume, choose on evidence.

    One brief, many variants, one number to watch.

    On the job social · POSM · campaign

    Machine learning 066 / 6

    Human check

    Every AI asset is checked like a vendor file.

    Spelling, logo geometry, colours, regional type, rights.

    On the job before anything is printed

    13 — Execution

    Design carried all the way to a finished, working, repairable sign.

    8 rules

    Execution 011 / 8

    Recce

    Measure, photograph, check power, check access.

    The recce sheet is the first page of the drawing.

    On the job day one, every job

    Execution 022 / 8

    Fabrication

    Cut, weld, powder coat, assemble. Four hands, four chances.

    One person signs each stage.

    On the job workshop

    Execution 033 / 8

    Installation sequence

    Datum, sub-frame, panels, letters, power, clean.

    Neighbours and mall rules decide the hour.

    On the job shop fronts · malls · sites

    Execution 044 / 8

    Fixing path

    Match the anchor to the wall before the board is made.

    When in doubt, add a fixing, not glue.

    On the job concrete · steel · glass · soffit

    Execution 055 / 8

    Modular rollout

    One kit, many shop widths; the fascia height stays fixed.

    Modular design is how a rollout stays on budget.

    On the job brand stores · multi-city

    Execution 066 / 8

    Site QC

    Level, colour, lighting, wiring, snag list.

    Sign off with a dated photo of every snag.

    On the job handover day

    Execution 077 / 8

    Warranty & AMC

    One year on work, three years on the structure.

    Leave access to the driver and one spare module on site.

    On the job service contracts

    Execution 088 / 8

    Handover folder

    As-built drawing, samples, photos, warranty, spares.

    If it is not in the folder, it is not in the job.
